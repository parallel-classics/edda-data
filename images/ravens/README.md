# images/ravens/ — Huginn and Muninn

Decision of 2026-11-01 (Grigory, through the cloud session): Huginn stands for search and the Names index, Muninn for
the reader's memory (progress, later notes). Both use one picture: John Gerrard Keulemans, *Corvus corax*, 1876
(hand-coloured lithograph from *Onze vogels in huis en tuin*; Commons `Corvus corax 1876.jpg`, public domain, the
artist died in 1912; Commons licence metadata checked 2026-11-01). Muninn is the same bird mirrored, so the two face
each other across the site bar; the mirroring is recorded per file (`mirrored`) and in the credit line.

Files (`index.json` lists sizes and the licence record): `huginn-320`, `huginn-640` and the Muninn twins (the whole
picture, 1× and 2×), `huginn-head-64`, `huginn-head-128` and the Muninn twins (head and beak with sky, for the round
badges at 28–32 px), each as JPEG and WebP. Made by `edda-site` with Pillow from the Commons original (not stored
here; the Commons page is the source). Credit lines: "John Gerrard Keulemans, 1876 · public domain" and
"John Gerrard Keulemans, 1876 (mirrored) · public domain".

## The feedback raven (task 35, 2026-11-14)

`huginn-fb-{day,dusk,night}-{128,256}.{jpg,webp}`: Huginn's round badge for the feedback button in the corner of every
page, one picture per light (the page picks it by the sun in the reader's time zone). Made by edda-site
`tools/make_feedback_raven.py`; the record is `feedback` in index.json. For now cropped from the Mímir portrait of
Huginn (images/portraits/huginn.jpg and its @dusk and @night relights), head and neck, mirrored to face into the page.
